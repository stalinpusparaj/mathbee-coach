Number Garden — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

  • We collect no personal information.
  • No accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases.
  • Everything stays on the device unless a parent chooses to export a backup.

Number Garden: Class 1 Maths (“the app”) is made by GEM CREATORS (gemmyapps@gmail.com). The app is designed for children aged 6–8 and their parents.

We collect no personal information

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services, no third-party software development kits, and it does not request internet access. No information about your child or your device is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on the device

To let your child continue where they left off, the app stores a nickname (optional), the chosen avatar, language, settings and learning progress in the app’s private storage on the device. This information never leaves the device unless you, the parent, choose to export a backup file using the parent area. That file is saved or shared only where you choose.

Speech

Spoken instructions use your device’s built-in text-to-speech engine, which runs on the device. The app does not record or listen to anyone; it does not use the microphone or the camera.

Android backup

If you have enabled Android device backup, Android may include the app’s private data in your own Google account backup, under Google’s policies. We never receive this data.

Deleting data

Use “Reset progress” in the parent area (behind the parent gate), clear the app’s storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. All data is then removed from the device.

Children’s privacy (COPPA, GDPR-K, UK Children’s Code)

Because the app does not collect, use, or disclose personal information from children, no parental consent is required. Settings default to the most private options. The parent area is protected by a parent gate.

Permissions

The Android app requests no Android permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever begins to collect any data, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before doing so.
